Welcome to Harpe Gaming

Harpe Gaming is a small board game store in the heart of Palmerston North, Manawatu. We carry a huge selection of strategy, card and family games, and we know every single one of them.

Samantha Goro, the owner of the store, has been playing board games since she was a child, and she is always happy to help customers find a game that suits their tastes.
