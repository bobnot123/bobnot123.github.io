Alliance

Rating: 5 / 5

Build a powerful empire in Alliance, a game of diplomacy and trade for three to six players, designed by the team at Corner House Games.

Every player controls one of six great houses. You make alliances, trade resources and send your armies out to win influence across the land. The twist is that your friends today can become your enemies tomorrow, so every promise has a price.

What I like

  • The negotiation between players keeps everyone involved from start to finish.
  • No two games ever play out the same way.
  • It fits well into an evening with friends — about two hours for a full game.

Things to watch out for

  • There is a lot of reading on the first play-through, so be prepared to learn the rules together.
  • You really need at least four players to see the game at its best.

Final verdict

Alliance is one of my favourite games this year. It is clever, tense and great fun, and it has earned a permanent place in the store. If you like games about negotiation and strategy, do not miss this one.
