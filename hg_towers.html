Towers and Temples

Rating: 4 / 5

Rule your own kingdom in Towers and Temples, a strategy game for two to four players from Beta Games.

In this game, each player takes on the role of a ruler who must build towers and temples across their land. You collect resources, lay down foundations and defend your territory, trying to finish your buildings before your rivals do.

What I like

  • The game is easy to learn but still offers deep strategic choices.
  • Each game takes about an hour, a good length for a week night.
  • The pieces are solid and the artwork is beautiful.

Things to watch out for

  • Some of the rule cards are a little hard to read at first, so have a quick look through them before you start.
  • It works best with four players.

Final verdict

If you enjoy building games with a bit of friendly competition, Towers and Temples is well worth a place on your shelf. I recommend it to anyone who likes games such as Carcassonne or Settlers of Catan.
